EIDO · from the Minho dialect

In the north of Portugal the eido is the land around the family house — the yard, the granite walls, the old trees. Twenty private residences and a fifteen-suite hotel, each house alone in a clearing of old chestnut trees.

The residences

Granite, bronze, oak.

Eighteen single-storey houses of 444 m² in an L, and two of 422 m² on two floors above the lake, on plots of about 3,000 m². Monolithic granite walls, a bronze-edged roof that reaches five metres past the glass, and 4.5 m glass that slides into the stone.

Outside

Life on the terrace.

Every house has a dining pavilion for twelve with a stone outdoor kitchen, a sunken lounge around a fire pit and a long black-stone infinity pool.

The hotel

A small hotel that serves the estate.

Two floors by the gate, an arrival court with a fountain and an eight-metre glass hall, a lounge and bar, a restaurant on the terrace, a spa with an indoor pool and a 25 m outdoor pool, a wine room, a library, a cigar lounge and a cinema. Suites of 62 to 80 m². The concierge looks after the houses too.
